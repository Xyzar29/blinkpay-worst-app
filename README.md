"The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched"
(`u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23`).

## The pitch

> BlinkPay is the app that charges you $0.99 every time you blink, and its
> $9.99-a-month premium tier generously lets you blink twice a day before it
> locks your eyelids until payday.

One sentence, 180 characters: a deadpan founder pitch for a product nobody
needs — a pay-per-blink fintech app with a premium tier that still barely
lets you blink.

## How it works

Run `main.py` (Python 3.13) with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set. It writes the
pitch above, and nothing else, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(mode 0644).

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
